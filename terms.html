03. Payment Terms (40/60 Structure)

Rushab Group follows a 40/60 payment structure for all projects:

  • 40% Advance Payment: The client must pay 40% of the total project amount before work begins.
  • 60% Final Payment: The remaining 60% must be paid after the project work is completed and before final delivery of the completed project.
  • Work will not begin until the advance payment has been received.
  • Rushab Group reserves the right to pause or withhold final delivery if the remaining payment is pending.
  • Any additional services or requirements outside the original agreement will be charged separately.

Rushab Group follows the following cancellation and refund policy:
